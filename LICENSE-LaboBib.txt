Licence of the LaboBib drawings
===============================

The laboratory apparatus drawings in this repository (pipeline/source/ and all device data
derived from them, including the device library used by the app) originate from the
freeware programs C-Design for Windows and the laboratory equipment library LaboBib.
They are NOT covered by the MIT licence in LICENSE, which applies to the source code of
Snapparatus only.

A few parts that LaboBib lacks (for example a reducer from NS 45 to NS 29) were put together for
Snapparatus from pieces of LaboBib drawings, only moved, combined or scaled to other standard
sizes (pipeline/scripts/derived.py, which names the drawings each one comes from). They are
adaptations of the LaboBib drawings and fall under the same terms as these, not under MIT.
